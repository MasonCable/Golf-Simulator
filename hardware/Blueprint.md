# Physical Launch Monitor

This is the blueprint for the basics of a launch monitor, we are trying to replicate the Foresight GC3 launch monitor. The initial budget for this project is $1,000.
This should be more than enough to cover the cost of a monitor that exceeds the capabilities of the GC3.

## Camera Layer (1)

For the machine vision cameras there are a few things that we need:

- Global Shutter
- External Hardware Trigger
- USB3
- High FPS at VGA-ish Resolution (or ROI Crop)
- Mono Prefered (Better With IR)
